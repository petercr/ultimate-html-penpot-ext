DejaVu Sans fixture font
========================

The bundled `DejaVuSans.ttf` is the unmodified DejaVu Sans font from the
DejaVu fonts project. It is included only so the offline visual fixtures have
a repeatable webfont. Its SHA-256 is:

ae7b7855e115a5966d8b1b3f80f254ccc117ec86f9965e202ee2940453837280  DejaVuSans.ttf
5c1247acef7f2b8522a31742c76d6adcb5569bacc0be7ceaa4dc39dd252ce895  DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf
